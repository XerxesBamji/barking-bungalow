Prices

We offer flexible care options to suit your dog's needs, whether it's a single walk, daycare, or a longer stay away from home. All services include attentive, experienced care in a safe and comfortable environment.

Pick Up / Drop Off Walks - £15

Perfect for busy days, our pick up and drop off walk service ensures your dog gets fresh air, exercise, and attention without you needing to worry about travel. Your dog will be collected from your home, taken for a supervised walk, and returned happy and settled.

Daycare – £32

Our daycare service provides a full day of care, play, and socialisation. Dogs enjoy structured playtime, rest periods, and plenty of supervision, making it ideal for dogs who enjoy company and routine while you’re out for the day.

Dog Boarding – £45 (per 24 hours)

Overnight boarding in a calm, home-from-home setting. Your dog will receive personalised care, regular walks, feeding to your routine, and plenty of affection, ensuring they feel comfortable and secure during their stay.

Holiday Rate – £50 per 24 hours (7 nights or more)

For extended stays of seven nights or more, our holiday rate applies. This ensures consistent, high-quality care throughout your dog’s stay, with extra attention given to maintaining routines and comfort over longer periods.
