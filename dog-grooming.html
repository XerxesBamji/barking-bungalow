Dog Grooming

The Bark Bungalow, where every dog is treated like family!

I am a passionate dog lover and built The Bark Bungalow to be more than just a grooming salon — it's a relaxing retreat for your furry friends. From soothing baths and breed-specific trims to gentle handling and plenty of cuddles. The goal is to make every pup look and feel their absolute best.

Fully qualified, first-aid trained, and fully insured — providing expert care and peace of mind with every groom. I have also trained in EMI Pet teeth cleaning which is an ultrasonic toothbrush system designed for dogs that uses gentle, motionless ultrasound to clean teeth and gums, eliminating the need for mechanical scrubbing, noise, or vibration.

At The Bark Bungalow, we understand that each dog has their own personality and needs, which is why we take the time to make every grooming experience calm, positive, and tailored just for them. Whether it's a simple tidy-up or a full spa day, we treat every visit with care, love, and a whole lot of tail wags.

Because at The Bark Bungalow… it's not just grooming — it's a lifestyle of love, comfort, and confidence.
