About Us

Welcome to The Bark Bungalow!

At The Bark Bungalow, based in Sully, Vale of Glamorgan, we pride ourselves on offering a safe and loving home-from-home experience for your furry companion. Jimmy, Sophie, and our own dog Ralphy form a dedicated, dog-loving team committed to ensuring your pet feels right at home.

We truly believe that when your dog stays with us, they’re not just a guest — they’re family. There’s nothing more rewarding than seeing a dog who doesn’t want to leave… and that always happen!

We’ve welcomed everything from tiny Mini Toy Poodles to gentle Newfoundlands, sleepy seniors to playful pups who can’t get enough cuddles — and we’re ready to care for your dog just the same.

Your Dog’s Safety & Comfort Are Our Priority

Our detached dormer bungalow has been thoughtfully designed with dogs in mind. With multiple rooms downstairs and three bedrooms upstairs, we can easily separate dogs if needed — while still ensuring they have garden access and plenty of comfort.

We’ve created dedicated dog-friendly spaces throughout the home. Our office, complete with cozy dog sofas, leads into a bright conservatory and out to a securely fenced and gated garden.

We only use DEFRA-approved cleaning products and operate strictly in accordance with our local authority licence requirements.

Licensed, Insured & Equipped

  • Fully licensed by the Vale of Glamorgan Council
  • Comprehensive insurance covering care, transport, and more
  • Food and medication are stored in clearly labelled, sealed containers
  • Daily sanitisation of bowls, bedding, and towels
  • Weekly deep-cleaning of our transport van
  • All rooms monitored with cameras and temperature control
  • Fresh water provided throughout the day

For added peace of mind, we’ve introduced Apple AirTags to help track your dog while they’re in our care. If your dog already wears a GPS tracker — great! If not, we’ll ensure they’re covered with one of ours (and their own name tag). We do ask that all dogs arrive wearing a collar.
